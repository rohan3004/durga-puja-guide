Bijoya Dashami

Wed, Oct 21

Sindoor Khela & Immersion.

  • Sindoor Khela

    Morning rituals where married women smear vermilion on the Goddess and each other. Maddox Square and Bagbazar are prime spots to witness this cultural phenomenon.

  • Bisarjan (Immersion)

    Head to Babu Ghat by late afternoon to watch the emotional procession and immersion of idols into the Hooghly River.

Pro Survival Guide

Transport

Rely exclusively on the Kolkata Metro. It runs almost 24/7 during Puja days. Cabs will get stuck in miles of barricaded traffic.

Footwear

You will walk literal marathons (15-20km a night). Wear your most broken-in sneakers. Absolutely no heels or new shoes.

Sustenance

The humidity combined with millions of people is intense. Carry a water bottle, wet wipes, and grab egg rolls from street stalls for quick energy!
